Why this mark

Barbers recognize it in half a second. The red-white-blue diagonal is the one symbol every barbershop already owns — it reads as "barber" before anyone reads a word.

It says the thing you sell. The pole is bent into a loop with an arrow: customers leave and come back. That's the whole pitch in one shape.

It survives being tiny. No thin lines, no text inside — it holds up as a favicon, a phone icon, an embroidered hat, or a sticker on a mirror.
