## 1. Kill the duplicate listing (biggest fix)

Search "tb solutions" on your phone. You currently get two listings: **Marketing agency**
and **Telemarketing service**. Two listings split every review, call and ranking signal
between them, and "Telemarketing" is the wrong category for who you are.

1. Open **business.google.com** (or search your business name while signed in) and see
   which listings sit under "You manage these profiles".
2. **If both are yours:** keep the Marketing agency one. On the Telemarketing one, open
   Business Profile settings → **Remove Business Profile**. Do not use "mark as closed".
3. **If the Telemarketing one is NOT yours:** on its Maps page tap **Suggest an edit**
   → **Report a problem** → **Duplicate of another place**, and pick the one you keep.
   Google reviews these in a few days.
4. Do not delete the verified one.

## 6. Reviews — you have zero

Reviews are the single biggest ranking lever on this list. Ask **real clients only**, every
one the same way, no incentives.

1. Business Profile → **Ask for reviews** → copy the short link.
2. Text each real client or past customer (hubs, DNE, anyone you've done work for):

> Hey [name], it's Nick from TB Solutions. If I've been helpful, would you leave a quick
> Google review? It takes a minute and really helps a new business: [link]. Thank you.

3. Reply to every review within a day, including the first.
