Texts to business owners who contact TB Solutions

If you are a plumber, HVAC or electrical shop owner and you fill out a form on tbsol.net and check the box that says "Yes, text me," Nick Byrd at TB Solutions will text you about your request: what he would do, what it costs, and follow-ups about that conversation. Roughly 1 to 5 texts per request, then only when you reply. These are one-to-one messages from Nick, not a marketing list. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to cancel at any time or HELP for help. Checking the box is optional; leave it unchecked and Nick calls you instead. We keep a record of the box you checked, the wording you saw, and the time, so you can ask what you agreed to.

Two separate programs for customers of the businesses we serve

Service call texts (from a business whose receptionist booked your call): booking confirmation, arrival window, "tech is on the way," and one follow-up after the job. About 2–4 texts per service call. You consent to these out loud on the phone when the receptionist asks, or by checking that box at tbsol.net/receptionist/sms-consent/.

Offers and reminders (promotional): seasonal maintenance reminders and occasional offers from that business, no more than 2 per month. These are never turned on by a phone conversation; they require checking that separate box at tbsol.net/receptionist/sms-consent/.

Each program has its own consent. Agreeing to one does not enroll you in the other, and you can book or buy anything without agreeing to either. Replying STOP cancels that business's texts to you.
