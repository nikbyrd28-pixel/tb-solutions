Real questions

Do I need sales experience?

No. Your kit has the exact words — the opener, what to say when they mention Booksy, what to do when they say "I'll think about it." Read it in the car, say it in the shop.

What does it cost me?

Nothing, ever. No kit fee, no inventory, no minimum. If you sign up zero shops, you owe nothing.

How much do I make?

You're paid per shop you bring in. Your exact rate shows on your dashboard the moment you join — check it there so you always see the current number.

When does a shop count?

When they're actually up and running — signed up on your link and using it with real customers. Signing up empty shops doesn't count for either of us.

How do I get paid?

We use the email you sign up with. Your dashboard shows what's owed and what's been sent.

Is there a quota?

None. Do one shop a month or ten a week. Nobody's calling you.
