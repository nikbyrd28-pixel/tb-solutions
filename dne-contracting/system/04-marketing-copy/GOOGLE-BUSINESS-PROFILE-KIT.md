## 7. Reviews — the script (she has 0; first 10 matter most)

Send this by text 1–2 days after a job is finished and paid, to every client, from her personal phone:

> Hi [name], it was a pleasure working in your home. If you have two minutes, a Google review really helps other women find someone they can trust with a remodel. Here's the link: [review link]. And if anything isn't right, tell me first and I'll fix it 🤍

Get the short review link: Google Business app → Home → "Ask for reviews" → copy. Put that URL in the site config (`googleReviewUrl`) so it also shows on the Reviews page and portal.

**Reply to every review within 24h.** Template for a 5-star:
> Thank you, [name]. Walking through your [bathroom] with you was the best part — glad it feels like yours now.

For anything below 5 stars: thank them, don't defend, say you'll call. Then call.
