Basement Remodeling questions we hear most

Can I add a bathroom to a basement with no plumbing?

Yes. Depending on the slab and the sewer line, we either cut a single pit for a sealed ejector or tie into existing drainage. Two quotes telling you to break up half the slab is often a sign to get a third.

Do you finish basements that have had water problems?

That is exactly where we start: we diagnose the water first and put the fix in writing before any framing or drywall.

Do you need a permit to finish a basement in Chester County?

In nearly every township, yes, and egress rules apply to bedrooms. We handle the paperwork and the inspections.
