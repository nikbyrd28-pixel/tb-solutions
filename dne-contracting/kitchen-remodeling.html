Kitchen Remodeling questions we hear most

How much does a kitchen remodel cost in Chester County and the 422 corridor?

It depends on layout changes, cabinet grade and how much plumbing or electrical moves. We never guess from a photo. After a free in-home visit you get a written estimate specific to your kitchen, with every line item explained. You can also use the online remodel planner for a ballpark before we visit.

Can you move my sink or add an island sink?

Yes. Relocating drains and supply lines is core plumbing work for us, so you are not paying a general contractor to subcontract the part that matters most.

Do I need a permit for a kitchen remodel in Chester County?

Usually yes once plumbing, electrical or structural work is involved. We pull the permits and schedule the inspections; you do not have to deal with the township.
