📍 Your Google listing

Google is where people search "barber near me". Do this once — it takes about ten minutes and it is free.

  1. Open business.google.com and claim your shop (or sign in if it is already yours).
  2. Paste the Website field below. Most shops leave this empty — that is the single biggest thing you are fixing today.
  3. Paste the description.
  4. Set your hours to match the ones below.
  5. Add your services and prices.
  6. Upload 5+ photos. Google shows shops with photos first.
  7. Post the first update below so the listing does not look dormant.
