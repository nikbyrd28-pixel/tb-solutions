What owners ask.

Is this SEO?

No. SEO takes months and a prayer. This is Google's pay-per-lead program. Once you're verified, you're eligible for the top spots immediately. Reviews and responsiveness decide how often you show.

Why not just run Facebook ads?

Facebook interrupts people who weren't thinking about plumbing. This catches people typing "plumber near me" with water on the floor. For service trades, search intent wins. Ads have their place, later, for remodel work.

I tried it and got junk leads.

Everybody does. About a quarter of leads are disputable, and Google refunds them if someone actually files. Nobody files. That's half of what the $147 is for.

Can I control what I spend?

Yes. You set a weekly cap with Google and can pause any time. Booked solid in May? We pause it and you pay Google nothing that week. The management fee just keeps running the months you run.

What do you need from me?

Your license number, insurance certificate, a 20-minute call, and patience for Google's background check. We do the rest.
