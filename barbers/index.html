What do I actually get?

One connected system for your shop: an online booking page, a loyalty rewards program, a referral engine, an AI tool that makes your content, and a tracker so no lead is missed. Use one piece or all of them.

Do my clients need an app?

No. Everything works from a link or a QR code at your counter — booking, rewards and referrals all open in the browser. Nothing to download.

Is this like Booksy? Can I keep it?

The booking piece does what Booksy does, but it's your own branded page — no marketplace showing your clients other barbers, and no per-booking cut. And you don't have to switch: keep Booksy if you love it and just paste your link — the rewards card's Book button opens your Booksy page. The loyalty, referrals and content all work on top either way.
