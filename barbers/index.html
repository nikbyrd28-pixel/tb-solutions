Questions

Straight answers

What do I actually get?

One connected system for your shop: a real website, an online booking page, a loyalty rewards program, a referral engine, an AI tool that makes your content, and a tracker so no lead is missed. Use one piece or all of them.

Do my clients need an app?

No. Everything works from a link or a QR code at your counter — booking, rewards and referrals all open in the browser. Nothing to download.

Is this like Booksy? Can I keep it?

The booking piece does what Booksy does, but it's your own branded page — no marketplace showing your clients other barbers, and no per-booking cut. And you don't have to switch: keep Booksy if you love it and just paste your link — your website's Book button opens your Booksy page. The loyalty, referrals and content all work on top either way.

How much does it cost?

You can try every piece live for free right now. When you're ready to run it for your shop, we set it up for you — tell us about your shop and we'll reach out.
