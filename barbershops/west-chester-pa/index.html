Where this list comes from, and what it isn't. It's built from OpenStreetMap — the open map database — filtered to independent barbershops within about three miles of the courthouse. Chains and hair salons are left out on purpose; this is about the shops where a man goes for a fade.

There are no star ratings and no "best of" order here, because we haven't sat in all eight chairs and inventing a ranking would be worse than useless. Shops are listed nearest-first from the middle of town.

Something wrong, missing, or closed? Map data goes stale and shops move. Tell us and it gets fixed — send a note here and we'll update it.
