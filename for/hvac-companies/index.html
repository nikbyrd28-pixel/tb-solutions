The calls you are missing

For an HVAC company, the ones that get away are no heat, no cooling, frozen lines, thermostat failures and furnace lockouts. They come in while you are already working, and the homeowner does not leave a voicemail. They call the next shop in the results.

What picks up instead

It answers in your company name, only after your own line rings out. It gets the problem, gives the safety step (the furnace switch and the thermostat batteries before anyone is dispatched), checks the zip is in your area, states your dispatch fee, and books into the arrival windows you actually run. You get a text with the name, address and the problem in their words.

It will not quote your work

It states your dispatch fee and nothing else. Pricing an HVAC repair over the phone is how you end up arguing in somebody's basement, so it says the tech prices it on site. Every time.
