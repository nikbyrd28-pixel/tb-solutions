# THE LAST GAME — EDIT, PACING, SOUND & RETENTION PLAYBOOK
### The rules the editor lives by (franchise-wide, tuned on Ep 101)

---

## 1. PACING LAW (the metronome)

- **Visual change every 5–10 seconds.** A cut, push-in, whip-pan, graphic pop, light shift, or B-roll cutaway. Set a marker track in the timeline every 8s; if a stretch has no change, it's a hole — fill it.
- **"Aha" reward every 20–30 seconds.** A fact lands, a scene turns, an image pays off. Map them (see §4).
- **Open a new loop the instant you close one.** Never let the viewer feel "resolved" — resolution = the exit.
- **No talking-head/static frame >8s** without a cutaway or move.
- **Act breaks are cliffhangers.** 3 of them (11:30, 24:00, 37:00), each ending on a smash-to-black + silence.

## 2. THE OPENING 30 SECONDS (most important 30s of the whole video)

- Frame 1 = the strongest image (Photo-1 SLAM). **No logo, no slate, no "welcome back."**
- State the payoff-promise (CIA + Compton) by ~line 2 so scrubbers know the stakes.
- First cut by 0:03. First on-screen text by 0:05. First loop open by 0:10.
- Target: <10% audience drop in first 30s (YouTube "intro retention").

## 3. RETENTION CURVE TARGETS (YouTube analytics goals)

**Swipe/scrub insurance:** every ~90s there's a striking, out-of-context image (battering ram, muzzle flash in eye, two-photo diptych, red-dot surveillance) so a scrubbing viewer re-hooks anywhere.

## 5. TRANSITION GRAMMAR (franchise vocabulary — use consistently)

- **Match-cut** = cause→effect / theme (praying hands → dice; casket-hands → cash-hands; baseball → kilo). Reserved for thesis moments.
- **Cross-fade grade migration** (warm↔cold within a shot) = the two-Americas thesis. Use sparingly (S19 is the hero).
- **Whip-pan** = energy/time jump (era morphs, act momentum).
- **Smash-to-black + silence** = trauma / act break / truth landing.
- **Slide-projector ka-chunk** = the framing device (cold open + callbacks).
- **Freeze-frame** = "hold on — you need to see this" hook.
- **VHS/CRT wipe** = entering "the record" (archival).

## 6. SOUND DESIGN BIBLE

- **The dice-rattle-into-silence** = the Pavlovian TRUTH cue. Only on real reveals (S2, S9, S12, S15, S17, S22, S23, S24). Overuse kills it.
- **Gunshots = sub-bass THUMP + silence + tinnitus**, never a Hollywood bang. Restraint = respect + more impact.
- **The "trauma dip":** at loss moments, duck the full mix to a muffled/underwater bus + tinnitus ring, then bloom back.
- **Music cuts OUT mid-bar** at the wealth→raid turn (S16→S17) — the jarring silence bills the audience for the high.
- **Silence is scored.** The three biggest beats (Norman's death, the CIA line, the acquittal-into-dread) play with little/no music.
- **Diegetic era-audio as narration:** Reagan "Just Say No" from a diner TV; Toddy Tee "Batterram" from a car — the era speaks for itself.

## 8. COLOR / FINISHING

- Build TWO show LUTs: **STREET-WARM** and **POWER-COLD**. Grade every shot into one; the migration shots blend both on a keyframed dissolve.
- Halation + gate weave + 35mm grain plate on ALL drama; VHS/CRT plate on ALL archival — never mix.
- The red-and-blue motif: keep police, gang, and flag reds/blues at the SAME hue values so the rhymes register subconsciously.
- Match the surveillance-sedan/red-dot color timing across S19/S21/S23 exactly (rewatch payoff).

## 9. LEGAL / ETHICAL EDIT NOTES (documentary standard of care)

- CIA / Iran-Contra material: frame allegations as the narrator's account and cite the public record (Gary Webb's *Dark Alliance*, the Kerry Committee report, the CIA Inspector General's Hitz report). Use "alleged / according to / he believes / the record shows" — it's both accurate and more credible.
- Real living people (Mark Wahlberg, Master P, etc.): depict only events described in the source memoir; secure life/appearance rights or cast look-alikes; add the standard "based on the memoir; some scenes dramatized" card.
- Do not glamorize: enforce the "every high pays its cost within the act" rule.
- Music/archival: clear Toddy Tee, N.W.A, Reagan/Iran-Contra footage, or use licensed sound-alikes / public-domain government footage.
- Add resource card in end credits (recovery / community reinvestment orgs) — franchise ethos is redemption.

## 10. DELIVERY SPECS

- Master: ProRes 422 HQ, 23.976, 2.39:1 within 16:9 (letterboxed) for YouTube; true 2.39 for festival/streamer.
- Loudness: -14 LUFS (YouTube), -16 LUFS (streaming apps), -19 dial-safe on the sub-bass hits (protect small speakers).
- Deliverables per episode: long-form master + .SRT/.VTT (EN/ES/PT), 1× Reel, 1× Short, 3× thumbnails, 1× community image, 1× 6s bumper.
