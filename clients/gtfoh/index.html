Questions

Before you call

How much does junk removal cost?

It's priced by how much space your stuff takes on the trailer, from a single item up to a full load. Text a photo and we'll send a real number. You'll always know the price before we start loading.

Do I have to bring everything to the curb?

No. That's the whole point. We'll go into the basement, attic, garage, or upstairs bedroom and carry it out ourselves.

Can you come today?

Often, yes. Same-day and next-day slots open up most weeks. Send the form or call and we'll tell you the soonest time.

Do I need to be home?

Not always. For curbside, driveway or garage pickups we can work out payment ahead of time and grab it while you're out.

What won't you take?

Hazardous materials like paint cans with liquid paint, chemicals, propane tanks, asbestos and medical waste. Almost everything else is fair game. If you're not sure, just ask.

Is it cheaper than a dumpster?

For most household jobs, yes. You skip the rental, the permit worries, the lawn damage, and the loading. You only pay for the space your junk takes up.
