Questions

Before you book

How much does junk removal cost?

It's priced by how much trailer space your stuff takes, from a single item up to a full load. Text a photo and we'll send a real number. You always know the price before we start loading.

Do I have to bring everything to the curb?

No. We go into the basement, attic, garage or upstairs bedroom and carry it out ourselves.

Can you come today?

Often, yes. Same and next-day slots open up most weeks. Send the form and we'll tell you the soonest time.

Do I need to be home?

Not always. For curbside, driveway or garage pickups we can settle payment ahead of time and grab it while you're out.

What won't you take?

Hazardous materials: liquid paint, chemicals, propane tanks, asbestos and medical waste. Almost everything else is fair game. If you're not sure, ask.

Is it cheaper than a dumpster?

For most household jobs, yes. You skip the rental, the lawn damage and the loading, and only pay for the space your junk takes up.
