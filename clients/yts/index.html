Questions

The ones everybody asks.

How long does it take?

About two minutes on the programmer once we're at the car. Cutting the blade and setting up is what fills out the visit — most jobs are done well inside an hour.

What's it going to cost me?

Spare keys start at $50 when you still have a working key. Programming runs $100–$200 depending on the vehicle, and the mobile service call is $100–$150. You get the full number on the phone before anyone drives out.

Do you actually come to me?

Yes — driveway, work parking lot, roadside, tow yard. That's the whole point: no tow bill to get the car to a dealer.

I lost every key. Am I stuck?

No. All-keys-lost is a normal job. We cut a new blade to your vehicle's code and program it on site. Have the year, make and model ready so we bring the right blank.

What cars do you work on?

Most domestic and import cars, trucks and SUVs — transponder, remote head, flip key and push-to-start smart fobs. Send the year/make/model and we'll confirm before we roll.

Do you need paperwork?

Yes. Your ID plus registration or title matching the vehicle, before any key gets cut or programmed. That's how a legitimate locksmith works — and it's what stops somebody else from doing this to your car.
