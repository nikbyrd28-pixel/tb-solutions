Questions

The ones everybody asks.

How long does it take?

About two minutes on the programmer once we're at the car. Cutting the blade and setting up fills out the rest — most jobs are done well inside an hour of pulling up.

What's it going to cost me?

Spare keys are $50 while you still have a working key. Programming runs $100–$200 depending on the vehicle, and the mobile service call is $100–$150. Full number on the phone, before anyone drives out.

What parts of Philly do you cover?

All of it — South Philly, Center City, Kensington and Fishtown, North and West Philly, Germantown, Mount Airy, Roxborough, Manayunk and the whole Northeast — plus Delco, Montco and Bucks.

Can you come to the airport lot?

Yes. PHL economy and garage lots are a regular call — people land after a week gone and the key isn't where they left it. Give us the lot and the row.

My car got towed and the key is gone.

We can work at an impound lot as long as the lot allows it and you've got the release paperwork and your ID. Call them for their access hours, then call us and we'll time it.

I lost every key. Am I stuck?

No. All-keys-lost is a normal job. We cut a new blade to your vehicle's code and program it on site. Have the year, make and model ready so we bring the right blank.

Do you need paperwork?

Yes. Your ID plus registration, title or insurance matching the vehicle, before any key gets cut or programmed. That's how a legitimate locksmith works — and it's what stops somebody else from doing this to your car.
