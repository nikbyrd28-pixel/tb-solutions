Our Story

Hubs & Babydoll began with a simple observation: the people who love the hardest often care for themselves the least.

The husband who keeps saying “I'm good,” while carrying the weight of providing. The mother who remembers everyone's appointments but forgets her own. The woman holding it all together while quietly running on empty. We've met them all — across our vendor tables, where “I'm just looking” so often turns into real conversation.

We're a small, home-based brand creating handcrafted self-care essentials and gift experiences designed to transform everyday routines into meaningful wellness rituals — one intentional moment at a time.

“Some of our favorite moments haven't been the sales — they've been the conversations.”

Whether it's a body butter that nourishes without the greasy feel, an oil that reminds someone they're appreciated, or a gift box that says “I see all that you do,” our hope is that each product becomes more than skincare. A pause. A breath. A reminder that your needs matter, too.

If you see us at a vending event, please stop by and say hello. Thank you for supporting our dream and letting Hubs & Babydoll become part of your rituals.
