What is in it

Four plant oils, vitamin E, and the scent. Nothing else.

Jojoba oil

The base of the blend. Close to the oil your skin makes on its own, so it absorbs easily.

Sweet almond oil

Softens rough spots and gives the oil its slip. Note: this is a tree nut oil.

Flaxseed oil

Rich in omega-3. Helps the oil hold moisture in longer.

Castor oil

Adds a light sheen. Good on beards and cuticles.

Vitamin E

Keeps the oil from going rancid in the bottle.

Scent

Jasmine & Gardenia, Bay Rum, Chocolate, or Peppermint.

How to use it

  1. After a shower, pat your skin so it is still a little damp.
  2. Put 3 to 5 drops in your palm and rub it where you want it. Start with less, you can add more.
  3. Give it about a minute to soak in, then get dressed.
  4. For beards, 2 or 3 drops worked in from the skin out. For cuticles, one drop per hand.

Keep the bottle out of direct sun. A 4 oz bottle used once a day lasts about two months.

Common questions

Will it leave my skin or clothes greasy?

Not if you use a few drops on damp skin and give it a minute. If it feels heavy, you used too much.

How much do I use?

3 to 5 drops per area on damp skin. A 4 oz bottle lasts about two months with daily use.

Which scent should I pick?

Jasmine & Gardenia is the floral one and a safe gift pick. Bay Rum is the men's scent. Chocolate is warm and sweet. Peppermint is cooling and good on feet and legs. Still unsure? Take the short scent quiz.

Is it safe for sensitive skin?

It is four plant oils and vitamin E with no added preservatives. It does contain sweet almond oil, which is a tree nut, so skip it if you have a nut allergy. Patch test first if you are not sure.
