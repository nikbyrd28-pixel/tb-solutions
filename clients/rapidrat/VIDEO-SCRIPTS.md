## How to film on your phone (one page, read once)

**Before you hit record**
- [ ] Wipe the camera lens on your shirt. Pocket smudge is the number one reason phone video looks cheap.
- [ ] Settings > Camera > Record Video: **1080p at 30 fps**. (4K is fine too, it just eats storage.)
- [ ] Turn on **Grid** (Settings > Camera > Grid). Keep your eyes on the top line.
- [ ] Hold the phone **straight up and down**. Vertical, every time.
- [ ] Phone on **Do Not Disturb** so a call doesn't kill the take.
- [ ] Wear the black Rapid Rat shirt and cap. Cap brim up a little so we can see your eyes.

**Light**
- [ ] Face the sun or the open sky. Never stand with the sun behind you (you turn into a shadow).
- [ ] Best times: morning or the last hour before sunset. Overcast days are great, no squinting.
- [ ] Black truck, black shirt: tap your face on the screen to lock focus and exposure, then slide the little sun icon up a notch if you look dark.
- [ ] Inside a garage or attic? Open the big door and face it, or turn on every light.

**Angle**
- [ ] Selfie shots: phone at eye level or a touch above, arm's length. Never from below the chin.
- [ ] Use the **back camera** for job footage (it's sharper). Use the front camera only for talking.
- [ ] Before/after shots: stand in the **exact same spot** both times. Pick a mark on the ground (a crack, a drain, your boot print).
- [ ] Prop the phone on the truck bed rail, tailgate, a bucket, or a cheap $15 tripod with a phone clip for hands-free shots.

**Audio**
- [ ] Step out of the wind. Stand with the truck between you and the breeze.
- [ ] Engine off, radio off, leaf blowers far away.
- [ ] Talk a little louder than normal, like you're talking to a guy across the driveway.
- [ ] Wired or Bluetooth earbuds with a mic help a lot when it's windy. Optional.

**Talking**
- [ ] Don't memorize. Read the line once, then say it your way. Contractions are good.
- [ ] Start talking the instant you hit record. No "um, okay." (Or trim it later.)
- [ ] Do 2 or 3 takes. Use the one where you sound most like you.
- [ ] Smile at the end of the line. Hold it for one second before you stop.

**Editing (CapCut or Instagram's editor, both free)**
- [ ] Cut every pause. Fast is good.
- [ ] Add **auto captions** on every talking video (most people watch on mute).
- [ ] Keep text in the **middle of the screen**. The top 15% and bottom 20% get covered by buttons and captions.
- [ ] Music low under talking, louder on the fast cuts. Pick from the app's library so it's licensed.
- [ ] Last frame: logo or truck + "Tap Get Quote, $25 off" + 484-245-4967.

**Post it**
- [ ] Post to Instagram as a Reel and share to Facebook. Also drop it in your Story.
- [ ] Caption: one line + "Tap Get Quote, $25 off" + phone number.
- [ ] Save the raw clips. We reuse them for ads.

## Script 1: Owner intro at the truck

**Length:** 20 to 30 seconds · **Filming time:** about 8 minutes · **Use:** the main ad, pinned to the top of your profile

**Hook (first 2 seconds)**
- Spoken: "I'm [your name]. I'm the Rat."
- On screen: **MEET THE RAT**

| # | Shot | Time | Spoken line | On-screen caption |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Front camera selfie, you standing at the driver side of the Silverado, cap on, truck behind you | 0 to 2s | "I'm [your name]. I'm the Rat." | MEET THE RAT |
| 2 | Same shot, you thumb back at the truck | 2 to 6s | "That's my truck. Rapid Rat Junk Removal, right here in Coatesville." | Rapid Rat Junk Removal · Coatesville, PA |
| 3 | Walk slowly along the side of the truck, still selfie | 6 to 13s | "Old couches, fridges, yard waste, garage and attic cleanouts, construction debris. If you don't want it, I'll haul it." | Furniture · Appliances · Yard waste · Cleanouts · Debris |
| 4 | Stop at the tailgate, drop it open with one hand | 13 to 18s | "Opened this year. Sixty-four Google reviews, all five stars. That means a lot to me." | 5.0 stars · 64 Google reviews |
| 5 | Lean on the tailgate, look right at the lens | 18 to 24s | "Got crap? Call the Rat. Neighbors get twenty-five bucks off right now." | $25 OFF Neighborhood Special |
| 6 | End card: logo or still of you and the truck | 24 to 27s | "Tap Get Quote." | Tap Get Quote, $25 off · 484-245-4967 |

**Filming tips**
- Light: park so the sun hits your face and the truck. Morning is best for a black truck (less glare).
- Angle: phone slightly above eye level, arm fully out so the truck fits behind you.
- Audio: engine off. If it's windy, stand on the side of the truck away from the wind.
- Do 3 full takes start to finish. Don't stitch lines; one clean walk feels more real.
- Music: optional, something light and upbeat at low volume.

## Script 2: Before / after with the flex

**Length:** 15 seconds · **Filming time:** about 5 minutes spread across the job · **Use:** the workhorse. Shoot one of these on every job you can.

**Hook (first 2 seconds)**
- Spoken: none, the pile does the talking. (Optional: "Watch this disappear.")
- On screen: **WATCH THIS DISAPPEAR**

| # | Shot | Time | Spoken line | On-screen caption |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Back camera, wide shot of the full pile from your marked spot. Hold still. | 0 to 2s | (optional) "Watch this disappear." | WATCH THIS DISAPPEAR |
| 2 | Phone propped on the tailgate pointing at the pile. You grabbing stuff and walking it to the truck. | 2 to 7s | none | (none, let it move) |
| 3 | Close shot of items landing in the bed | 7 to 9s | none | none |
| 4 | Back to your marked spot. Same frame as shot 1. Empty, swept. Slow push forward. | 9 to 11s | none | GONE. |
| 5 | Front camera, you in front of the empty spot, quick flex, grin | 11 to 13s | "Got crap? Call the Rat." | Got crap? Call the Rat! |
| 6 | End card | 13 to 15s | none (or "Tap Get Quote.") | Tap Get Quote, $25 off · 484-245-4967 |

**How to get the fast cuts:** prop the phone and just let it record the whole load-up. In the editor, chop it into 4 or 5 half-second pieces and speed them up 2x to 4x.

**Filming tips**
- Light: shoot "before" and "after" from the same spot in the same light. If the sun moves behind a cloud, wait 30 seconds.
- Angle: chest height, phone straight. Get the whole pile in frame with a little floor showing on every side.
- Audio: music carries this one. Pick something with a hard beat and cut on the beat.
- Always ask the customer if it's cool to film their place. Keep house numbers, plates, and faces out of frame.
- Caption the post with the town and the job type (example: "Garage cleanout in Caln").

## Script 3: "What's in the truck today" walk-around

**Length:** 20 seconds · **Filming time:** about 4 minutes · **Use:** Stories, plus a Reel once or twice a week. Easy to make every day.

**Hook (first 2 seconds)**
- Spoken: "Here's what the Rat picked up today."
- On screen: **WHAT'S IN THE TRUCK TODAY?**

| # | Shot | Time | Spoken line | On-screen caption |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Front camera selfie at the back of the loaded truck | 0 to 2s | "Here's what the Rat picked up today." | WHAT'S IN THE TRUCK TODAY? |
| 2 | Flip to back camera. Slow pan across the load, start at the tailgate. | 2 to 6s | "We got a recliner..." | (label each item as you say it) |
| 3 | Keep panning, point at the next item | 6 to 10s | "...an old dryer, a bunch of busted shelving..." | Dryer · Shelving |
| 4 | Pan up to the top of the pile | 10 to 14s | "...and about six bags of yard junk." | Yard waste |
| 5 | Flip back to selfie | 14 to 17s | "All before lunch. What've you got sitting around?" | What's in YOUR garage? |
| 6 | End card | 17 to 20s | "Tap Get Quote." | Tap Get Quote, $25 off · 484-245-4967 |

**Note:** the items above are examples. Say what's actually in the truck that day. That's the whole point.

**Filming tips**
- Light: shoot with the sun at your back for the pan shots (so the load is lit) and turn to face it for the selfie bits.
- Angle: stand on the bumper or a step to get a little above the load. Looking down into the bed shows more.
- Audio: talk while you pan. If you stumble, keep going and trim later.
- Ask a question at the end. Comments push the video to more people.

**Length:** 15 seconds · **Filming time:** about 5 minutes · **Use:** Black Friday through December ads

## Script 6: "Got crap?" skit with the rat

**Length:** 15 seconds · **Filming time:** about 8 minutes · **Use:** comment bait, shares, and people tagging their buddy with the overflowing garage

**Props:** the Rapid Rat banner OR a rat mask. Plus one sad pile (an old chair, a box of junk, anything).

**Hook (first 2 seconds)**
- Spoken (you, as the homeowner, staring at the pile): "I really gotta deal with this..."
- On screen: **POV: the couch has been on the curb for 3 weeks**

| # | Shot | Time | Spoken line | On-screen caption |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Phone propped. You, no cap, staring at the junk pile, hands on hips | 0 to 2s | "I really gotta deal with this..." | POV: the couch has been on the curb for 3 weeks |
| 2 | Hard cut: the rat (you in the mask, or the banner) pops into frame from the side | 2 to 4s | (in a rat voice, or just a squeak) "Got crap?" | GOT CRAP? |
| 3 | Homeowner you, jump back | 4 to 5s | "Ahh! Who are you?" | none |
| 4 | Rat points at the pile, then at the truck | 5 to 8s | "Call the Rat." | CALL THE RAT! |
| 5 | Sped-up 3x: pile going into the truck, rat waving bye | 8 to 12s | none | (fast music) |
| 6 | You, cap on, normal, by the empty spot, shrug at the camera | 12 to 13s | "He's not wrong." | none |
| 7 | End card | 13 to 15s | "Tap Get Quote." | Tap Get Quote, $25 off · 484-245-4967 |

**No mask?** Hold the banner up so the rat covers your face for shot 2 and 4, or film the banner on the truck and zoom in fast with a "dun dun DUN" sound.

**Filming tips**
- Light: shoot it all in one spot, same light, so the cuts look clean.
- Angle: lock the phone on a tripod or the tailgate. Don't move it between shots, just you moving in and out of frame. That's the trick that makes it look edited.
- Audio: say the lines big and goofy. The rat voice can be dumb. Dumb is good here.
- Keep it to one joke. Don't explain it.
- Pin a comment: "Tag the guy whose garage needs the Rat."
