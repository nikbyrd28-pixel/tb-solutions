# Rapid Rat Junk Removal: Referral Partner Plan

Draft for review. Nothing in here has been offered to anyone. Every dollar amount and term below needs the owner's explicit OK before it goes out (see "Needs sign-off" at the bottom).

**Goal:** cheap, repeat local jobs that fill the gaps between Meta ad leads. Ads bring one-off homeowners. Partners bring the same kind of job over and over.

**What we know about him (only use these facts in outreach):** one truck (Chevy Silverado), owner-operator, Coatesville PA 19320, opened in 2026, 5.0 stars from 64 Google reviews, $25 off Neighborhood Special, 484-245-4967. Services: junk hauling, furniture and appliances, yard waste, garage and attic cleanouts, construction debris.

## 3. The referral offer

**Draft offer: $25 to the partner for every completed, paid job they send, plus priority scheduling for their own jobs.** The customer still gets the $25 Neighborhood Special, so the partner can tell people "mention me and you save $25."

Why this shape: it's a flat amount he only pays after he gets paid, so it can never cost him on a job that falls through. It's recurring, since a PM who sends 3 turnovers a month earns every month. Confirm $25 makes sense against his average job price before offering; if his typical job is small, start at $20 or offer priority scheduling only.

**Terms (5 bullets):**
- **What counts:** a new customer who books and pays for a job and was sent by the partner. Partner's own properties count too. Repeat jobs from the same customer count for 12 months from the first job.
- **How it's tracked:** every partner gets their own link (`[site URL]/?utm_source=partner&utm_content=CODE`, code up to 16 characters, like `nhp` or `remaxace`). The quote form already saves this code on the lead. For phone or text referrals, the partner texts 484-245-4967 with the customer's first name and street before the job, so there's a time-stamped record. No text or link, no payout. No "they said your name" deals.
- **When it pays:** once a month, by the 10th, for jobs completed and paid the month before, with a short text listing each job (date, street, amount). Payment method to be set by the owner.
- **Priority scheduling:** partner jobs get the next open slot ahead of new ad leads when possible. It's not a guaranteed time. One truck means some days are full, and he'll say so up front.
- **Exit:** either side can end it any time with a text. Jobs already booked before that text still pay out. No contracts, no minimums, no exclusivity on either side.

**Two-way, no-money version (thrift and donation centers, HOAs):** he drops off usable items he picks up, they give out his card for things they can't take. For HOAs, offer residents the existing $25 off on cleanup days instead of a per-job fee.

## Needs sign-off before anything goes out

1. **The $25 partner payout** (or a different amount, or priority scheduling only). Check it against his average job price.
2. **How and when payouts are sent** (monthly by the 10th is a draft).
3. **The 12-month repeat-customer window.** Shorter is cheaper; longer is more appealing to PMs.
4. **The priority scheduling promise.** He has to be comfortable bumping ad leads for partner jobs.
5. **Timing promise.** No same-day claim is in these scripts. If he can reliably do same or next day, he can add it himself.
6. **Partner codes:** someone has to set up each link and check the leads table monthly to match codes to paid jobs.
