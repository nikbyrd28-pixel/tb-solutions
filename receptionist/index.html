Questions

What owners ask.

My customers want a real person.

They want a person who picks up. Right now they get voicemail. Call the demo line, (610) 998-6138, and decide whether you'd hang up on it. If anyone asks, it says plainly that it's your automated assistant and that it can book them now.

Do I have to change my phone number?

No. You keep your number, your ads, your truck lettering. We set up conditional forwarding so only the calls you don't answer, and after-hours calls, roll to the receptionist. Your line rings first, every time.

How long does setup take?

One 20-minute call with Nick: hours, service area, fees, what not to book, how you want emergencies handled. The line is live within 48 hours, and anything you want changed afterward is a text to Nick, not a ticket.

What happens on an emergency call?

You set the rule. Transfer it live to your cell, offer same-night dispatch at your after-hours fee, or take a detailed message and promise a 15-minute callback. Either way you get the text with the address and the problem while they're still on the line.

My wife handles the phones.

Then this is for the calls she can't get: nights, weekends, when she's on the other line. It only answers after your line rings out, so she stays first.

What if it books something wrong?

It only books into windows you gave it, with the capacity you set. Anything unusual becomes a message to you instead of a booking. Emergencies ring your cell.

Does it quote prices?

It states your dispatch fee and nothing else. For everything else it says the tech will give an exact price before any work starts.

I already have an answering service.

Does it book the job, text the customer a confirmation, and text you the details within a minute? If yes, keep it. If it takes a message and emails you in the morning, you're paying $200–400 a month to lose the job slower.

I run Housecall Pro / Jobber.

Good. Paste your Housecall Pro API key during setup and every job it books shows up on your HCP schedule with the customer, address, arrival window and dispatch fee already filled in. Jobber is next; until then the job is texted to you and lives in your dashboard.

Is there a contract?

No. Month to month. Cancel by texting Nick. Your phone number stays yours.
