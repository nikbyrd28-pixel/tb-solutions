Questions

What owners ask.

I already have a website.

Does it text you the quote request within a minute of someone filling the form? If yes, keep it. If it's a brochure, this replaces it, or sits on a new domain next to it and we send your ads there.

Do I need this if I have the receptionist?

They cover different doors. The receptionist catches people who call. The website catches people who search first, and most homeowners under 50 look you up before dialing.

Why one page?

Because a homeowner with water in the basement doesn't browse. One page, one decision: call or request a quote. More pages mostly give people places to leave.

Who owns the domain and the site?

You do, both. If you ever cancel, I hand over everything and it keeps running wherever you host it.

Can you do more than one page?

Yeah. Town pages and service pages for search are an add-on once the main page is earning. Start with the one that books jobs.
