## F. CAPTION / SUBTITLE STYLE (accessibility + retention)

- Font: bold condensed sans (e.g., Montserrat ExtraBold), white, 90% opacity black stroke/shadow.
- Position: lower third for long-form (2.39:1 letterbox bar); CENTER 4:5 safe for Reels/Shorts.
- Timing: 1–2 lines max, ~32 chars/line, break on natural speech pauses.
- Emphasis: key words (CIA, $300K, ACQUITTED, NORMAN) in **red** or scale-pop for 6 frames.
- SDH: include `[dice rattle]`, `[sub-bass thud]`, `[sirens]`, `[silence]` — the sound design IS the story; caption it.
- Burned-in for social; sidecar .SRT + .VTT for the long-form master (multi-language ready: ES/PT priority given the subject matter's reach).
